Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about how the app behaves.

How does ReStock calculate stock?

Each product holds a stock count. Sales subtract from it, purchases and received reorders add to it, and adjustments set or change it.

How are low-stock products detected?

A product is Out of Stock at 0 units, Low Stock at or below its reorder level, and Healthy above it.

How do sales affect inventory?

Recording a sale reduces stock by the quantity sold, then updates alerts, the dashboard and stock history.

How do purchases affect inventory?

Recording a purchase increases stock by the quantity received and logs the movement in stock history.

What are reorder levels?

The stock count at which you want to replenish a product. Set it per product when you add or edit it.

What are AI insights?

Simple demo calculations, such as stock divided by daily sales, that suggest what to reorder. They are examples, not production AI.

Managing Products

  1. Open Products
  2. Use + Add Product or Edit
  3. Set reorder level and supplier
  4. Check the status badge

Recording Sales

  1. Click Record Sale
  2. Pick product and quantity
  3. Review remaining stock
  4. Save to update inventory

Recording Purchases

  1. Click Record Purchase
  2. Choose product and supplier
  3. Enter quantity and price
  4. Save to add stock

Creating Reorders

  1. Open Alerts
  2. Click Create Reorder
  3. Confirm suggested quantity
  4. Mark Received to add stock
