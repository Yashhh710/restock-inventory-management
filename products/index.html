Why Choose ReStock?

ReStock brings everyday inventory tasks into one simple workflow, helping small retailers track what they have, identify what needs attention, and act before products run out.

  • Simple inventory tracking built for everyday retail.
  • Automatic stock updates from sales and purchases.
  • Clear alerts and reorder insights before stock runs out.
