Services & How It Works

A simple inventory system built to help small retailers stay organized, avoid stockouts, and know when it’s time to reorder.

ReStock brings everyday inventory tasks into one simple workflow. Add products, set opening stock and reorder levels, record sales and purchases, and automatically keep track of current stock. When products reach their reorder level, ReStock highlights them with low-stock alerts so retailers can take action before shelves become empty.

From product management and stock tracking to sales history, supplier information, reorder management, reports, and AI-powered inventory insights, ReStock connects each step of the inventory process in one place.

Track what you have. Understand what is moving. Get alerted when stock is low. Reorder before you run out.

Keep Your Inventory Organized

Add products, set opening stock, define reorder levels, and keep your inventory information in one place.

Includes:

  • Product management
  • Opening stock
  • Categories
  • Reorder levels
  • Supplier information

Record Every Stock Movement

Track sales and purchases to keep your current inventory updated without manually recalculating stock.

Workflow:

  • Purchase → Stock In
  • Sale → Stock Out
  • Current Stock = Updated Automatically

Know Before You Run Out

ReStock monitors stock levels and highlights products that reach their reorder level.

Alerts:

  • 🟡 Low Stock
  • 🔴 Out of Stock
  • 📦 Reorder Needed

Turn Inventory Data Into Useful Insights

Analyze sales and inventory patterns to identify fast-moving products, potential stockout risks, demand trends, and possible reorder recommendations.

Important: Since this is currently a student demo, phrase it as “AI-powered insights” rather than claiming production-grade prediction accuracy.

From Low Stock to Reorder

When products reach their reorder level, ReStock helps organize the next step by keeping supplier information and reorder details accessible.

Workflow:

Low Stock → Review → Reorder → Receive → Stock Updated
